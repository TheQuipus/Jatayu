# Jatayu Connect - Chapter 1: The Decision

## Creative Platform

**The Human Signal** turns uncertainty into a visible object. A vermilion filament begins as a quiet loose thread, accumulates pressure, becomes a dense decision knot, receives one warm human pulse, and resolves into a clear path leading to the next chapter.

The 3D is narrative infrastructure, not decoration. It should always communicate one of four states: uncertainty, pressure, recognition, or clarity.

## Global Design Language

### Palette

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#080A10` | Primary spatial background and deepest shadows |
| Warm ivory | `#F1EEE7` | Atmospheric light, live copy, moments of clarity |
| Vermilion | `#D63618` | The human signal and primary brand action |
| Oxidized copper | `#A66B45` | Junctions, warm rim light, tactile detail |
| Mineral teal | `#2F7770` | Maximum 5% of a frame; depth separation only |

### Materials

- Human signal: hundreds of satin vermilion fibers with slight physical variation.
- Decision membranes: smoked glass, thin bevels, controlled refraction, never mirror chrome.
- Junctions: tiny brushed copper nodes with restrained highlights.
- Environment: matte carbon-black spatial volume with warm ivory haze and sparse dust.
- Grain: fine 35mm-style grain at low opacity; never a distressed overlay.

### Lighting

- Key: large cool soft source from upper-left, approximately 5600K.
- Rim: narrow warm source from lower-right, approximately 3200K.
- Volumetrics: broad and subtle; preserve true blacks and negative space.
- Bloom: limited to the active signal and pulse; no global glow wash.
- Teal: only a faint bounce in the deepest background planes.

### Composition

- Primary format: 16:9 landscape, designed for 3840 x 2160 delivery.
- Keep the active sculpture in the right 55% for the opening website state.
- Reserve the left 35-40% for accessible live HTML copy and CTAs.
- Use real depth: foreground fiber, hero object, atmospheric background.
- Avoid centered mandalas, decorative symmetry, busy star fields, and generic sci-fi UI.

### Typography And UI

Typography must remain live in the website, never baked into generated imagery. Use expressive sentence-case display type for the emotional line and a highly legible grotesk for product UI. Motion should reveal words by line or phrase, not by rapidly animating every character.

### Motion Grammar

- Base movement is slow, deliberate, and physically plausible.
- Camera acceleration and deceleration use smooth cinematic ease-in/out, never elastic bounce.
- The signal may pulse at 0.6-0.8 seconds per wave.
- Orbiting fragments should move at different shallow depths and no faster than 12 degrees per second.
- Scroll reversal must reverse the state cleanly without discontinuous cuts.
- Reduced-motion mode replaces camera travel with three static dissolves: loose thread, decision knot, clear path.

## 30-Second Professional Video Generation Prompt

Create a 30-second, 3840 x 2160, 16:9, 24 fps cinematic 3D brand film titled **The Decision**. Maintain one continuous world and one consistent sculptural object throughout. The visual language is premium editorial 3D: ink-black spatial volume, warm ivory atmospheric haze, vermilion satin fibers, smoked-glass membranes, tiny brushed-copper junctions, restrained mineral-teal depth, deep blacks, controlled volumetric light, subtle 35mm grain, physically based materials, realistic depth of field, and restrained bloom.

**00:00-00:04 - Quiet uncertainty.** Begin on a wide 24mm lens at eye level, camera approximately 5 meters from the subject. A nearly empty black volume contains soft ivory haze entering from upper-left. A single dim vermilion filament drifts in from frame-right and hangs unresolved in the right half of frame. Dolly forward at approximately 0.25 meters per second. Keep the left 40% quiet for live website copy. Motion is almost imperceptible.

**00:04-00:09 - Pressure builds.** Seamlessly change toward a 35mm field of view while continuing the forward move. Seven to ten thin smoked-glass fragments and four small copper nodes enter at different depths and begin a slow inward orbit around the filament. Add an 8-degree clockwise camera yaw. The filament curls into several loops; tension increases without violent motion. Preserve the same upper-left cool key and lower-right warm rim.

**00:09-00:14 - The knot.** Resolve into a 50mm three-quarter hero view. Arc the camera 30 degrees clockwise around the object while the loose fibers form a dense suspended knot inside fractured smoked-glass membranes. Copper nodes become connection points. The knot remains right of center and fully visible. Increase local contrast and material detail; do not add extra objects or interface graphics.

**00:14-00:18 - First human pulse.** Use a motivated continuous push into an 85mm macro view. Track laterally with one warm copper-white pulse as it enters a vermilion strand, crosses a brushed-copper junction, and briefly illuminates nearby fibers from within. Use shallow depth of field with the active junction sharp and the recognizable knot soft behind it. The pulse is elegant and warm, not electrical lightning.

**00:18-00:24 - Release.** Pull back to a 40mm lens and rise slightly above the knot. Orbit 35 degrees counterclockwise with a 5-degree downward tilt. As the pulse travels, the knot loosens naturally: loops separate into directional strands and glass fragments settle into calm ordered layers. Warm ivory illumination expands from lower-right while the cool key remains. Let the movement breathe; no sudden morph or explosion.

**00:24-00:30 - Clear next move.** Transition to a wide 28mm view and pull back slowly. The sculpture is now open, calm, and asymmetrical. One luminous vermilion filament forms a clean confident path from the resolved object toward bottom-center, visually inviting the next scroll chapter. Gently tilt down by 6 degrees to follow the path. End on a quiet hold of at least 12 frames with warm ivory light opening the darkness and the left side still available for live copy.

**Camera and render constraints:** smooth gimbal-like movement; realistic motion blur at a 180-degree shutter; no handheld shake; no fast zoom; no whip pan; no fisheye distortion; no cuts that change object identity; maintain consistent scale, materials, and lighting across all shots.

**Negative constraints:** no text, logo, watermark, people, faces, literal bird, map of India, phone mockup, floating UI, holographic interface, purple-blue cyberpunk palette, neon overload, chrome sphere, decorative blobs, fantasy magic, lightning, explosion, excessive lens flare, oversaturated orange, or generic AI-gloss aesthetic.

**Optional sound direction:** low restrained room tone, soft filament tension, delicate glass movement, a warm tactile pulse at 00:15, and a subtle resolving breath from 00:24. No epic trailer hit and no voice-over in this chapter.

## Quality Gate

- The same knot remains identifiable in every frame.
- The left-side copy area stays readable at desktop and laptop crops.
- Vermilion remains the only dominant accent.
- Glass edges remain legible against black without looking chrome.
- The ending path clearly creates downward scroll momentum.
- The concept works as static imagery when motion or WebGL is unavailable.
